Features

designed specifically for easy visual navigation and quick information retrieval.
•	USER-CENTRIC NAVIGATION:

Why Use Tabular Form?
•	Ideal for long, complex reports covering multiple distinct concerns.

The Five Standard Components

•	FINDINGS:
Presents the objective facts, raw data, and evidence discovered during the investigation.

•	CONCLUSIONS:
Offers a critical interpretation and objective analysis of the findings.

•	RECOMMENDATIONS:
Proposes specific, actionable steps or solutions derived from the conclusions.
